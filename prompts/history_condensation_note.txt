Chat history condensation marker:

When a conversation grows too long, each specialist's filter_messages() function inserts a
condensation SystemMessage as a placeholder for the trimmed middle section.

Two variants exist in the codebase:

VARIANT A — ENT, Psychiatry, Pharmacy (ent_logic.py:591, psych_logic.py:585, pharmacy_logic.py:680):
Trigger: len(other_messages) > 40 (keep first 15 + last 25)
Message text:
  [SYSTEM NOTE: {middle_count} middle messages have been condensed to save memory.
  The earlier clinical history and patient details are still accessible.]

VARIANT B — Dermatology (dermatologist_logic.py:1745):
Trigger: len(other_messages) > 10 (keep first 2 + last 8)
Message text:
  [Earlier messages condensed; all demographics and findings are preserved in clinical state]

In the dermatology specialist, confirmed demographic and clinical state facts are re-injected on
every turn via build_system_message(profile), so the condensation threshold is tighter (10 messages
vs 40 messages) because the SystemMessage already carries all known patient data redundantly.
