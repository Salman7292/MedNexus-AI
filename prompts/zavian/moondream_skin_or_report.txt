## Prompt: Comprehensive Clinical Dermatological Examination & Image Assessment

Perform a detailed clinical examination and assessment of this image:
1. Anatomical Location or Object Category:
   - Where on the human body is this located? (Be specific: e.g. nose, cheek, forehead, chin, periorbital / eye area, lips, ear, neck, abdomen, chest, back, arm, hand, leg, foot).
   - If this is a medication, tablet, pill, capsule, medicine packaging, blister pack, syrup bottle, or cream/ointment tube, state 'medication or tablet' and identify the medicine/product name if readable.
   - If this is a printed paper lab test, biopsy report, or medical document, state 'medical document'.
   - If non-human object/animal, state 'other'.
2. Detailed Visual Findings:
   - For skin photos: describe specific lesions (macules, papules, pustules, comedones/blackheads, patches, plaques), color (erythema/redness), borders, and surface texture (scaling, crusting).
   - For medications/tablets: describe tablet shape, color, imprint, brand/generic drug name, dosage/strength (e.g. 500mg, 1%), formulation (tablet, capsule, topical cream, ointment, gel), and packaging details.
   - For medical reports: extract patient name, date, test names, impressions, and diagnoses.
3. Suspected Condition or Medication Clinical Role:
   - For skin photos: what dermatological condition or pattern does this visual presentation most strongly suggest?
   - For medications: what is this medication typically used for (e.g. antibiotic, antifungal, corticosteroid, antihistamine, acne treatment) and how does it relate to skin therapy?
