## Prompt: Comprehensive Clinical Dermatological Examination

Perform a detailed clinical dermatological examination of this image:
1. Anatomical body part / location: Where on the human body is this located? (Be specific: e.g. nose, cheek, forehead, chin, periorbital / eye area, lips, ear, neck, abdomen, chest, back, arm, hand, leg, foot). If this is a printed paper lab test or report, state 'medical document'. If non-human object/animal, state 'other'.
2. Detailed visual findings: Describe in comprehensive detail what is on that location: specific lesions (macules, papules, pustules, comedones/blackheads, patches, plaques), color (erythema/redness, depigmentation, hyperpigmentation), borders, surface texture (raised, flat, rough, scaling, crusting), and pore features.
3. Suspected dermatological condition / pattern: What specific skin condition, diagnosis, or clinical pattern does this visual presentation most strongly indicate or resemble (e.g., acne vulgaris / comedones, vitiligo, melasma, eczema, rosacea, folliculitis, tinea, etc.)?

---
NOTE: Passed to the Moondream VLM via model.query(image, exam_prompt).
The prompt extracts (1) specific anatomical location, (2) detailed description of what is on that location, and (3) suspected dermatological condition/presentation.
Results feed into create_image_analysis_json() which constructs structured clinical observations including `what_is_on_location` and `suspected_condition_patterns`.
